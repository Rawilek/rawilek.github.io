✨ What is syncflow?

🛡️ Privacy and Security, in Plain English

syncflow uses real end-to-end encryption. That phrase gets used loosely, so here is what it means in practice: the scrambling and unscrambling of your data happens on your devices, using keys that only your devices hold. Nobody in the middle — not the network operator, not a neighbour on the same Wi-Fi, not the people who built syncflow — can read the contents of what you send.

  • No cloud storage. Your files are never uploaded to a server, so there is no copy to be leaked, subpoenaed, or sold.
  • No accounts. You are never asked for an email address, a phone number, or a password to a remote service.
  • Local-first design. The app is built to work entirely on your own network, and it keeps working when the internet does not.
  • Zero-trust pairing. Devices have to be paired deliberately before they can exchange anything, so a stranger on your network cannot simply help themselves.

❓ Common Questions

My other device is not showing up.

In most cases the two devices are on different networks, or your router isolates wireless clients from one another (a setting often called "AP isolation" or "client isolation"). Confirm both machines are on the same Wi-Fi name, then check the router settings if the problem continues. A quick restart of syncflow on both sides also clears up most pairing hiccups.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. syncflow works entirely over your local network. You can transfer files between two laptops on a plane, in a basement, or anywhere else without a signal.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no built-in cap. The only limits are the free space on the receiving drive and how long you are willing to leave the machines connected. Interrupted transfers resume rather than restart.

Where do received files go?

Into the download folder you selected during setup. You can change this at any time from the settings screen, and each incoming transfer shows you exactly where it will land before you accept it.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, go to Apps, find syncflow in the list, and choose Uninstall. Your transferred files stay exactly where they are — uninstalling only removes the application itself.
